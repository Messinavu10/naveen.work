In progress

An agent that learns

Most AI agents start every conversation from scratch. When someone corrects them, or they finally solve a tricky problem, that lesson disappears the moment the chat ends.

I’m building a platform that lets agents hold on to what they learn. It picks up on what worked, what didn’t, and what people corrected, turns that into knowledge the agent can use, and brings it back the next time a similar situation comes up. People stay in the loop, reviewing anything the system isn’t sure about.

The goal is simple: agents that get better the more they’re used, whatever framework they’re built on.

It’s in active development with a small team, so the code isn’t public yet. I hope to share more about it soon.
